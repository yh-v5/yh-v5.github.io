Abstract

ISLPED ’26

An algorithm–hardware co-design that makes HDC encoding fully compatible with sparse adder trees (SATs) on digital SRAM-CIM arrays. Replacing XOR-binding with AND-binding compounds sparsity; sparsity-tuned projection vectors push bound vector sparsity above 0.99; adaptive binary quantization compensates for the non-uniform output statistics that AND-binding induces. On 128x64 SRAM-CIM arrays, HyperSPACE reduces encoding energy by up to 13.03x while improving classification accuracy by up to 3.82% across four benchmarks.

† Co-corresponding authors: K. E. Jeon and J. H. Ko.
